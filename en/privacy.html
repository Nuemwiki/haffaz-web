Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This is an English translation of the Turkish original; if there is any difference between the two, the Turkish version applies.

This policy explains what information the Haffaz AI mobile application (iOS and Android) and the haffaz.net website use and for what purposes. You do not need to create an account, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use Haffaz AI. This document has been prepared in consideration of the Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK — Turkish Personal Data Protection Law) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller is the developer of Haffaz AI. For your questions and requests regarding your data: destek@haffaz.net

2. Audio recordings (Identify and Find the Recitation)

When you want to identify a verse, the short audio recording (approximately 10 seconds) initiated by you is first analyzed within the app (Identify) on your device, without being sent to the internet. If the device cannot be certain of the recited verse or if the recording was made via the Find the Recitation shortcut, the recording is sent to our server and to a third-party AI service that we use for verse recognition to identify the verse. The audio is used solely for this analysis; it is not stored on our server, and temporary files are deleted once the analysis is complete. The microphone is not used unless you initiate listening.

3. Location

If you grant location permission, it is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for your location. In Turkey, your location is converted into a province/district name and prayer times are retrieved from the Diyanet (Presidency of Religious Affairs) website; outside Turkey, coordinates are sent to the Aladhan prayer times service to retrieve prayer times. Your location is not sent to or stored on our server. If you prefer, you can also use the app without granting location permission by selecting a province/district.

4. Anonymous identifier for daily analysis allowance

To track the free daily analysis allowance, the app generates a random identifier on your device. This identifier contains no information that identifies you. Only this identifier and the number of analyses performed on that day are kept on the server; records from previous days are deleted. Deleting the app also deletes this identifier.

5. Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the application, we anonymously measure which features are used and how often; for example, "app opened", "Identify used and Al-Baqarah 255 found on device", "Quran opened, 5 pages read", "'patience' searched in Search". The search queries you enter in Search and topic search, along with the verses identified in Identify, are included in these statistics to see the most frequently searched words and verses. Along with these records, only the aforementioned random identifier, the device's operating system, and the app version are sent. Name, email, phone number, audio recording, or precise location are not sent; screen recording and automatic tap tracking are not performed. The analytics service can only infer approximate country/city information from the connection's IP address. This data is not used for advertising or tracking purposes, is not sold, and is stored on the European Union servers of our statistics service provider.

6. Advertisements

The app displays advertisements via Google AdMob; for this purpose, advertising identifiers and ad interactions are processed by Google. On iOS, permission is requested for ad tracking; if you do not grant permission, non-personalized ads are displayed. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a consent window for ad preferences; you can change your preference within the app under About → Ad Privacy Options. AdMob's data usage is subject to Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Technical logs for security purposes

To protect the service against abuse (such as sending excessive requests), our server temporarily retains the IP address from which requests originate in memory (at most a few hours); it may also appear in our hosting provider's system logs for a limited time. This information is not used for any other purpose.

8. Notifications

Prayer time, pre-prayer, and verse of the day notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Your notification settings are stored solely on your device.

9. Information stored on your device

Your favorite verses, saved locations, analysis history, downloaded recitation files, dhikr counts, and app settings are stored solely on your device. Deleting the app also deletes this information.

10. Sharing of information and international data transfer

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with third parties necessary to provide the services described above, and solely for the relevant purpose:

  • Third-party AI service provider for verse recognition
  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Our server hosting provider (Render)
  • PostHog (EU servers) for anonymous usage statistics
  • Diyanet (Presidency of Religious Affairs) and Aladhan for prayer times
  • Quran.com, AlQuran Cloud, jsDelivr, GitHub, EveryAyah, and Internet Archive for Quran text, translations, and recitation files; these services may technically see your IP address when downloading files

Some of these services have servers located abroad; therefore, your data may be processed abroad.

11. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK and GDPR, you have the right to learn whether your data is being processed, request information, request correction or deletion, object to processing, and withdraw your consent. You can change your microphone and location permissions, as well as your ad tracking preferences, at any time through your phone's settings. For your requests, you can email destek@haffaz.net.

12. Security

All communication between the app and our server is carried out over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Audio files sent to the server are subject to size and duration limits and are deleted after analysis.

13. Children

Haffaz AI is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that information belonging to a child is being processed, please contact us.

14. haffaz.net website

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools. The verse of the day is retrieved from the same server as the app. Fonts are loaded via Google Fonts.

15. Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page. You can also review our Terms of Use.
